Naya Article — Kab, Kaise, Spam Na Ho Iska Dhyan

Kab Publish Karein

  • Tue / Thu / Sat, shaam 7 baje (Indian audience sabse active)
  • Hafte me zyada se zyada 3 — roz-roz daalna spam lag sakta hai
  • Publish ke baad 24 ghante, 7 din, 28 din par yahi dashboard par number dekho

Google Ko Spam Na Lage

  • Har article ka maksad alag — wahi purani lines ghuma ke mat daalo
  • Keyword ghusana nahi, baat samajh aani chahiye
  • Apne asli example, photo, tajurba daalo
  • Title me wahi likho jo andar sach me hai

Natural Review (Publish Se Pehle)

  • Ek baar zor se padhke dekho — bolchal jaisa lage
  • Robot jaisi line ho to insaan jaisi banao
  • Tareekh, daam, jankari dobara check karo
  • 3-5 sahi jagah par apne dusre page ke link do
  • Photo apni/asli ho, uspar sahi naam (ALT) likho

Agent Ke Liye Kaam Ka Tareeka (padhne ke liye) 
1. Roz pehle taza GA4 + Search data lo, is dashboard ko update karo.
2. Jo page kamzor hai ya traffic gira hai, use Kaam Ki List me sabse upar rakho.
3. Naya article: topic pehle duplicate check, phir likho, natural review, SEO 85+, apni image, tabhi publish (Tue/Thu/Sat 7 PM). Spam wala kaam bilkul nahi.

5. Koi password/API key is page me kabhi mat likho — wo agent ke private paas hai.
