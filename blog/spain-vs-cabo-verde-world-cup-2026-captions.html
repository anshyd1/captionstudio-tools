Match Preview — What to Expect

On paper this is a mismatch: Spain are priced as overwhelming favourites and have scored two or more goals in eight of their last ten matches. But Cabo Verde have quietly built a reputation as a tough, organised side — they held both Egypt and Iran to draws during qualifying and kept clean sheets doing it. A repeat of Curaçao’s consolation goal against Germany would be celebrated like a trophy across the islands.

For your content, the smart move is to have both outcomes ready: a La Roja dominance caption and a Cabo Verde pride caption. Whatever happens at Mercedes-Benz Stadium, you’ll be first to post.
