❓ FAQ — Real Sawal

Gujarat Titans ka motto kya hai?

Gujarat Titans ka official war cry "Aava De!" hai — jiska matlab Gujarati me "Let's Go!" hota hai. Ye team ki signature line hai jo fans har match me chillaate hain.

GT ne IPL kab jeeti?

Gujarat Titans ne apne debut season 2022 me hi IPL trophy jeeti — pehle hi season me champion! 2023 me runners-up rahi. Ye IPL history ka rare achievement hai.

GT ke top players kaun-kaun hain?

Hardik Pandya (Captain), Shubman Gill (Star Opener), Rashid Khan (World #1 Spinner), Mohammed Shami (Pace), aur Sai Sudharsan (Rising Star) GT ke main heroes hain.

Gujarat Titans ki jersey ka color kya hai?

GT ki primary jersey color Navy Blue + Golden Yellow hai. Yellow color GT ki identity ban chuki hai.

GT vs CSK matches kahan dekhe?

JioCinema (free) aur Star Sports (TV) par dekh sakte ho. Highlights aur memes ke liye hamari dedicated GT vs CSK Memes blog me 50+ funny captions hain!

GT ke captain kaun hain 2026 me?

Shubman Gill 2024 se GT ke captain hain, aur 2026 season me bhi unhi ki leadership me team khel rahi hai. Hardik Pandya 2024 me Mumbai Indians chale gaye the.

GT ka home ground kaunsa hai?

Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad — duniya ka sabse bada cricket stadium (1,32,000 capacity)! Ye GT ka home ground hai.
