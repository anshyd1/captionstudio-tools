What CaptionStudio is

CaptionStudio is a free Instagram toolkit built and maintained by one person — Ansh Yadav, in Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh. It has three generators (caption, hashtag, bio) and 34 written guides in English, Hindi and Hinglish. There is no signup, no paywall, and no account required to use anything on the site.

The project started in early 2026 because most "caption generator" sites at the time either demanded an email address, hid the copy button behind an ad, or returned the same five generic lines for every input. CaptionStudio was built as the version we wanted to use ourselves.

How the site stays free

CaptionStudio is funded by display advertising. That is the entire business model — no paid tiers, no data selling, no affiliate-only pages dressed up as guides.

Ads are served through Google AdSense and are kept deliberately light:

✔️ No pop-ups, no interstitials, no auto-playing video
✔️ No ads inside the generators (the tools you came for stay clean)
✔️ No "click to reveal the caption" walls
✔️ You can decline analytics and ad cookies and still use every tool

If ads ever stop covering the running costs, the tools stay online — they are static pages with no per-user cost.

What actually helps the project

The most useful things readers do are free, and they matter more than money:

1. Tell us what is broken. A dead link, a caption that reads wrong, a generator returning something silly — send it to us through the contact page and it gets fixed, usually the same week.

2. Share the guide you used. If a caption pack worked for you, link it to a friend who makes Reels. That is how the site is found.

3. Give feedback on the guides. If a guide did not answer your question, say so. Guides are rewritten based on reader questions.

4. Turn off your ad blocker here, if you keep one. It is a fair ask — the site has no other income and the ads are not intrusive.

What we do not do

To be clear about the boundaries of this project:

We do not sell follower growth, engagement, verification or "guaranteed reach". We do not run paid courses, and we do not promise anyone will earn money from Instagram. Guides about creator income describe how work is priced and invoiced in the market — they are not income promises, and any site that promises a fixed monthly figure from Instagram is selling you something.
