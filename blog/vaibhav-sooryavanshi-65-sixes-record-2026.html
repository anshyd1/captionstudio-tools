Kaise Bana Wo Historic Moment

27 May 2026, Maharaja Yadavindra Singh Stadium, Mullanpur. IPL 2026 ka Eliminator — SRH vs RR. Pat Cummins ne toss jeeta aur RR ko batting di. Vaibhav opening pe aaye aur pehle 6 overs me hi 60 runs kuta diye.

Jab Vaibhav 53 sixes pe tha — Gayle ke 59 ke record se sirf 6 dur — pura stadium pe sirf ek awaaz thi. "Aaj record tootega!" Aur phir 60th, 61st, 62nd, 63rd, 64th aur 65th six — sab ek hi innings me. 14 saal purana record dhwast. 97 runs 29 balls me. 12 sixes, 5 fours. Strike rate: 334.48.

🤯 Mind-Blowing Fact

Jab Gayle ne 2012 me 59 sixes ka record banaya tha, Vaibhav sirf 1 saal ka tha! 14 saal baad, wahi bachha 15 saal me record tod deta hai — aur Gayle ne khud applaud kiya.
