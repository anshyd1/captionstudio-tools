What is an Instagram stylish name?

An Instagram stylish name is a profile name written in a more attractive, unique, or memorable style. It can be used as your display name, Instagram stylish username, page name, nickname style, or brand identity.

Stylish names work best when they are easy to read. A clean name like “Royal Soul” often looks better than a long line full of unreadable fonts and symbols.
