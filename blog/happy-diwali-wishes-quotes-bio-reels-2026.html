How to Make Your Festive Ethnic Reel Go Viral in 4 Simple Steps

Use this creator-tested checklist before posting ethnic transition Reels, Dev Deepawali ghats clips, or wedding-season carousels:

STEP 1

Shoot Near Warm Point Lights (Diyas / Fairy Lights)

Turn off harsh white tube lights and stand 3 feet in front of warm yellow fairy lights for natural DSLR background bokeh.

STEP 2

Lock the First 1.8 Seconds With Movement

Start with lighting a diya, adjusting a jhumka, or a dupatta swirl rather than a static standing frame.
