Viral Caption Formula – Instagram Growth Specialists ka method

200+ accounts dekhne ke baad hamne pattern catch kiya. Har viral caption me 3 cheezein common hoti hain. Yahi formula hum apne clients ko recommend karte hain:

  1. Hook line (3-5 word): Emotion ya curiosity gap. "Tumhe pata hai...", "Honest baat...", "Aaj realize hua..." – ye starters work karte hain.
  2. Personality line (3-5 word): Aapki actual stance. Generic mat raho. "Mujhe yeh nahi pasand", "Main isko reject karta hoon" – specific.
  3. Engagement trigger: Question ya CTA. "Tum kya karte ho?", "Tag karo us dost ko jo..." – direct ask.

Sample: "Honest baat – main late night Reels banata hoon. Quality milti hai. Tum kab post karte ho?" – hook + personality + question. Tested formula.

Caption Length ka science

  • Reels entertainment: 8-12 words
  • Reels educational: 15-25 words
  • Reels emotional: 30-40 words max
  • Carousel posts: 50-80 words
  • Stories: 5-7 words
Pro tip: Caption me apna primary keyword pehle 50 characters me daalo. Instagram Search aur Google dono is part ko index karte hain. Niche jo 500 captions hain – sab is rule pe banai gayi hain.

5 caption mistakes jo Reels viral nahi hone dete
