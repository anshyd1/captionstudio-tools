How to Choose the Best VIP Name & Username

With thousands of options, picking the right one is easier with a simple formula. Here’s how Indian creators choose names that actually get followers:

  • Match your niche & vibe. A luxury/brand page suits a royal name (Royal Highness), while a username works as @vip.soul. Your name should preview your content.
  • Keep it short & memorable. Names under 15 characters are easier to remember, type and search. Avoid random numbers like Priya123456.
  • Add one symbol or emoji — not five. A single ✨, 👑 or a fancy ꧁ ꧂ from the symbols box makes it stylish without looking cluttered.
  • Use a Desi or regional touch. Words like Munda, Raja, Singh or your city (Delhi, Mumbai) help local followers connect faster.
  • Keep it consistent across apps. If you also use YouTube or Pinterest, use the same name for brand recognition.

Pro tip: remember the Name field is different from your @username — you can change the Name anytime and it does not have to be unique, so feel free to experiment with the names above.

FAQ — Instagram VIP Name & Username

What is the best Instagram VIP name & username in 2026?

VIP names work best when they feel exclusive and premium — like King Of Insta, Royal Highness, @vip.soul or महाराजा. Add a 👑 or a dot to make it unique. They are relatable, memorable and stand out in Instagram search. Add a dot, emoji or fancy symbol to make it unique.

How do I put a stylish name on my Instagram profile?

Tap any name on this page to copy it, open Instagram, go to Edit Profile, paste it into the Name field (not the username), and save. You can edit the spelling or add symbols before saving.

What is the difference between Instagram name and username?

The username is your unique @handle that must be unique. The Name is the bold display line under your photo — that is where stylish names, Hindi names, emojis and symbols go, and it does not need to be unique.

Can I use Hindi or symbol names on Instagram?

Yes. Instagram supports Hindi (Devanagari) names and fancy Unicode symbols in the Name field. Just tap to copy from the Hindi or Premium Symbols section and paste into Edit Profile.

How do I add a heart or symbol to my Instagram name?

Scroll to the Premium Symbols box above, tap a symbol like ❤️, ꧁ or ✦ to copy it, then paste it before or after your name in the Edit Profile Name field — for example, ❤️ Pretty Soul ❤️.

How do I make a 2-line stylish name on Instagram?

Instagram’s Name field is single-line, so a true two-line name is not supported there. For two lines, use the Bio field instead and add a line break, or keep your stylish name in the Name field and put the second line in your bio.

How many times can I change my Instagram name?

You can change your Name (the display name) up to twice within 14 days. Your @username can be changed too, but frequent changes may confuse followers, so pick one you like and stick with it.
