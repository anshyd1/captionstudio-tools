🛡️ 7-Point Student Safety Checklist: How to Spot Fake Earning & Telegram Task Scams

Cybercriminals actively target college students with WhatsApp messages offering "₹150 per Google Maps 5-star review" or "Like 3 YouTube videos to get ₹200." They pay ₹150 once to win your trust, and then trap you into "Prepaid VIP Tasks" stealing ₹5,000–₹50,000. Memorize and share these rules across your college WhatsApp groups:

  1. Zero Upfront Deposit Rule: Legitimate companies deduct platform fees (if any) from your earned payout — they NEVER ask you to UPI money to unlock a task or withdraw your balance.
  2. Avoid Unlisted `.apk` Downloads: Never install "earning mod APKs" sent via WhatsApp or Telegram; they read SMS OTPs and drain bank accounts.
  3. No Colour Prediction, Aviator, or "Guaranteed Signal" Gambling: Real-money prediction games mathematically wipe out student savings and carry severe financial & legal risks.
  4. Never Rent Out Your Bank Account or SIM Card: Offering your student bank account for "commission" makes you a mule in cyber-fraud investigations.
  5. Verify GST / CIN & Play Store Developer History: Platforms like Internshala, CashKaro/EarnKaro, GroMo, and Google have verifiable Indian corporate offices and millions of authentic reviews.
  6. Keep UPI PIN Strictly Secret: Entering a UPI PIN is ONLY required to send money, NEVER to receive a scholarship, cashback, or freelance payment!
  7. Report Scams Immediately on 1930: If anyone in your hostel falls for an online scam, dial the National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in within the golden first hour.
