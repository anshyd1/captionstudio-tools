Best viral hooks follow this simple 3-part structure. Pehli line mein yeh teeno elements hona chahiye.

Hook Formula = [Curiosity / Emotion / Controversy] + [Specific Benefit / Pain / Story] + [Promise / Twist / Question]

The Real 3-Tier Hashtag Strategy (15 Max — Stop Using 30 Random Ones)

Old way (2024-25): 30 hashtags, all high competition. Dead in 2026. Algorithm ab spam samajhta hai.

2026 winning way (exact formula top creators use):

Tier 1 (High Volume - 5 tags): 1M+ posts. For broad discovery. Example: #Reels #InstagramReels #ViralReels #ContentCreator #IndiaReels
Tier 2 (Medium - 5 tags): 100K-1M posts. Your niche sweet spot. Example: #HinglishCaptions #ReelsIndia #SmallTownCreator #IndianCreator #ViralHooks

07 · The System

Complete 7-Step Viral Caption System (Use This Every Time)

Step 1: Hook in first line (curiosity/emotion/controversy + specific + promise)
Step 2: Story or value in next 2-3 lines (Indian context, personal touch)
Step 3: Relatable line (your audience ka pain ya win)
Step 4: 3-tier hashtags (5 high + 5 medium + 5 low = 15 max)
Step 5: Strong CTA (save, tag, comment, share)
Step 6: Post at best time (use your analytics or 7-9 AM/PM IST)
Step 7: Reply to every comment in first 60 minutes (algorithm gold)
