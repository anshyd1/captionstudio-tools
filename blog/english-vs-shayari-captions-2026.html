Pro Tip

Pro Tip — Caption Kaise Use Kare

English line ko hook banao, shayari ko emotional punch. Instagram algorithm ko reveal-twist format pasand aata hai kyunki viewer second line ka wait karta hai — watch time improve hota hai.

  • English line = Hook: first 1-2 seconds me simple line dikhao.
  • Shayari = Emotional punch: beat drop ya photo transition par shayari reveal karo.
  • Reveal twist: text overlay me “But in Shayari…” likhne se curiosity badhti hai.
