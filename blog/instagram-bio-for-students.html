Make your bio feel like you

Start with what you do or enjoy, add one detail that shows your personality, and keep the wording easy to read. For example: Design student 🎨 | Learning every day | Chai enthusiast ☕. Check the character limit and preview on your own device before saving.

Should I use my college name?

Only if you are comfortable sharing it publicly. You can say “college student” instead and leave out details about your location or routine.

Can I change these examples?

Yes. Mix Hindi and English, replace the emoji, or write a version that matches your own interests. These are ideas, not a promise of more followers.
