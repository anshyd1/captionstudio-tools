RCB ne pehli IPL trophy kab jeeti?+
RCB ne 2025 IPL Final me Punjab Kings ko 6 runs se harakar apni pehli IPL trophy jeeti — 2008 se 18 saal ke intezaar ke baad. Match Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad me hua. RCB score tha 190/9, PBKS 184/7 tak hi pahunch saka.
Virat Kohli ne trophy jeetne par kya kiya?+
Virat Kohli trophy jeetne par ghutno par gir gaye aur unke chehre par aansu aa gaye — 18 saal ki mehnat aur 267 matches ka safar ek pehli trophy me badla. ESPNcricinfo ne likha: "Kohli fell to his knees and cupped his face in his arms, shedding a few tears."
RCB kitne IPL finals haaree hai?+
RCB 2025 se pehle 3 IPL finals haaree — 2009 (Deccan Chargers se 6 wickets se), 2011 (CSK se 58 runs se), aur 2016 (SRH se 8 runs se). 2016 final me Kohli ne 973 runs (IPL record) banaye the par trophy nahi mili.
Virat Kohli IPL me kab se khel rahe hain?+
Virat Kohli IPL ke pehle season 2008 se khel rahe hain — sirf RCB ke liye. 267 matches, 8,661 runs (all-time #1), 8 centuries (record). Wo ek bhi IPL match kisi aur franchise ke liye nahi khele — ye cricket history ki sabse badi loyalty story hai.
RCB 2026 back-to-back jeet sakti hai?+
IPL history me sirf CSK (2010-11) aur MI (2019-20) ne back-to-back titles jeete hain. RCB ne 2026 Qualifier 1 jeeta — GT ko 92 runs se harakar. Last 8 IPL finals me Qualifier 1 winner ne hi trophy uthaayi hai.
