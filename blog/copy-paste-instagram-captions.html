🏆 Caption Likhne ke 7 Pro Tips (2026)

Sirf captions copy karna kaafi nahi. Viral captions banane ka formula samajhna padega. Neeche hain 7 proven tips jo India ke top creators use karte hain:

  1. Hook pehli line mein daal: Instagram "...more" se pehle sirf 2-3 lines dikhata hai. Pehli line itni strong honi chahiye ki log click karein.
  2. CTA zaroor daal: Har caption ke end mein ek action — "Comment karo", "Save karo", "Tag karo". Engagement 2x guaranteed.
  3. Emojis strategically use karo: Bullet points ki jagah emojis use karo. 2-3 relevant emojis kafi hain — zyada mat daalo.
  4. Length sahi rakh: Reels ke liye 150 chars, posts ke liye 300-400 chars, informative posts ke liye 500+ chars.
  5. Hashtags pehle comment mein: 2026 mein experts kehte hain hashtags comment section mein daalo — caption clean lagega.
  6. Sahi time par post karo: India mein best time: Tue-Fri, 11am-1pm ya 7pm-9pm. Consistency sabse important hai.
  7. Story-based likho: Sirf photo describe mat karo — ek micro-story banao. "Gaya tha, socha yeh, hua woh" — people connect karte hain stories se.
