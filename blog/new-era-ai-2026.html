❌ Section 03

Level 1 — Beginner Prompt (Ye 90% Log Karte Hain)

Ye prompt wo hai jo most beginners likhte hain. Simple, direct, aur completely lacking in context. Result dekhoge toh samjhoge kyu itne log frustrated rehte hain AI se.

L1 Beginner Prompt
😴 Basic
Give me an online earning idea.

📊 AI Output

😐 "Start freelancing on platforms like Fiverr or Upwork"
😐 "Create a blog and earn through advertising"
😐 "Try affiliate marketing by promoting products online"
😴 Ye Google bhi de deta — completely generic, vague, kisi ke bhi kaam ki nahi
🔍 Analysis — Kya Problem Hai?
  • No role defined — AI ko nahi pata kisk perspective se boluon
  • No audience — student ke liye alag, professional ke liye alag
  • No context — India ya global? Zero investment ya capital available?
  • No format — kitne ideas? kitni detail? kaunsa structure?

Result: AI ne apna "safest" response diya — jo literally useless hai. AI ka dosh nahi — prompt ka dosh hai.

Level 2 — Intermediate Prompt (Better… But Still Average)

Is level pe log thodi more specific hone ki koshish karte hain. "5 ideas do" — thoda better. "Simple and practical" — ek direction. Lekin still kafi cheezein missing hain. Output dekho:

L2 Intermediate Prompt
🙂 Better
Give me 5 online earning ideas for beginners. Make them simple and practical.

📊 AI Output

🙂 Freelancing — offer writing, design, or social media services on Fiverr
🙂 Affiliate Marketing — promote products and earn commissions
🙂 Online Tutoring — teach subjects you know on platforms like Unacademy
🙂 Content Creation — start a YouTube channel or Instagram page
🙂 Selling Digital Products — create eBooks or templates
🙂 Better… structured hai, 5 ideas hain — lekin still generic, koi specific steps nahi, koi India-specific angle nahi
🔍 Analysis — Kya Better Hua, Kya Miss Hai?

Better hua: 5 numbered ideas, slightly more structured.

Still missing:

  • No target audience — Indian student vs UK professional — same output?
  • No actionable steps — "start freelancing" is not a step
  • No AI angle — 2026 mein AI tools use nahi kiye prompt mein
  • No zero-investment filter — capital available hai ya nahi?

Ye output padh ke client nahi milega. Ye padh ke business nahi chalega. Thoda better hai — lekin actionable nahi.

⚙️ Section 07

Hidden Formula — RAGF System (Jo Pro Prompt Mein Tha)

Level 3 prompt mein exactly kya tha jo ise itna powerful banaya? Ye ek 4-part formula hai jise hum RAGF kehte hain. Ye yaad karlo — ye tumhara lifetime prompt engineering cheatcode hai:

🔑 The RAGF Formula — Har Prompt Mein Use Karo

R Role AI ko batao — kaun ban ke baat kare? Expert, teacher, strategist?
A Audience Kiske liye? Indian student? Working professional? Beginner?
G Goal Kya result chahiye? Earning? Growth? Learning? Specific output?
F Format Kaisa output? Kitne points? Language? Tone? Word limit?
Level 3 Prompt Ka RAGF Breakdown: R → "Act as an online earning expert specializing in AI tools for India" A → "Target: Indian students with zero investment available" G → "Suggest 5 beginner-friendly earning ideas using AI" F → "Explain each in 2 lines with clear actionable first steps"
🔥 RAGF = Har Prompt Ko Instantly Pro Level Pe Le Jaata Hai

Ye formula sikhne mein 5 minutes lagte hain. Apply karne mein 30 seconds lagte hain. Aur result difference — 10x hota hai. Guaranteed.

Copy-Paste Prompt Pack — Universal Aur Advanced (Aaj Se Use Karo)

Ab do universal prompts jo tum kisi bhi topic ke liye customize kar sakte ho. Inhe template ki tarah use karo — brackets mein apni info fill karo, done.

UNIVERSAL PROMPT — Any Topic, Any Goal 
Role:    Act as an expert in [FIELD — e.g., "online business", "content creation", "fitness"].

Task:    Give me 5 practical ideas for [GOAL — e.g., "earning online", "growing Instagram", "losing weight"].

Audience:Target: [WHO — e.g., "Indian college student with no money", "working professional with 1 hour daily", "complete beginner"].

Format:  Explain each idea in 2-3 simple sentences.
         Include 1 clear first action step for each.
         Language: Hinglish. Keep it practical and honest — no hype.

// Simply replace [brackets] with your specific topic
// Works for: earning, study, fitness, business, content, skill learning
ADVANCED PROMPT — Strategic Planner 
Role:    Act as a strategic planner with expertise in [FIELD — e.g., "digital marketing for India", "AI-powered freelancing"].

Task:    Create a step-by-step 30-day action plan for [GOAL — e.g., "starting AI freelancing", "growing to 10K Instagram followers"].

Audience:Target: [WHO — e.g., "Indian student, complete beginner, zero budget"].
         Constraints: [LIMITATIONS — e.g., "only 2 hours per day available", "no investment"].

Format:  Week-by-week breakdown (Week 1, 2, 3, 4).
         Each week: 3-4 specific daily tasks.
         End with: expected results after 30 days.
         Be realistic — no false promises.
         Language: conversational Hinglish.

// This prompt works for literally any 30-day challenge or goal
// Output: A complete roadmap you can actually follow

  • Content: Field = "Instagram growth", Goal = "reach 10K followers", Audience = "creator, 0 followers"
  • Fitness: Field = "home workout", Goal = "lose 5kg in 2 months", Audience = "busy student, no gym"

⚠️ Section 10

Biggest Mistake + Quick Fix — 90% Log Ye Isliye Fail Karte Hain

90% log yahi galti karte hain — aur fir AI ko blame karte hain

Test ne clearly prove kar diya. Ye 3 mistakes hain jo 90% beginners karte hain — aur isliye result weak aata hai:

  1. Random Prompts — No Structure
    Jo mann mein aaya wo type kar diya. Koi role nahi, koi audience nahi, koi format nahi. AI ne apna safest, most generic response diya. Fix: RAGF formula use karo — hamesha, without exception.
  2. No Clarity — Vague Instructions
    "Kuch accha de do" — AI ke liye "accha" undefined hai. AI ne wo diya jo statistically most people ke liye "accha" hota hai — generic. Fix: Specific banao. Kitna? Kaisa? Kiske liye? Kitne words?
  3. First Output Pe Final — No Iteration
    Pehla output liya aur use kar liya — without any refinement. Best outputs hamesha 2nd ya 3rd iteration pe aate hain. Fix: Pehle output ke baad: "Make it more specific / shorter / emotional / India-focused" — refine karo.
🔥 The Honest Truth

AI bahana nahi hai. AI ne wahi kiya jo tumne bola. Agar tum vague the — AI vague tha. Agar tum specific the — AI specific tha. Problem AI mein nahi, problem input mein hai.

Input improve karo → output improve hoga. Simple math. Aaj se RAGF formula use karo — result kal se different hoga.

🚀 Section 11

Upgrade Rule — Aaj Se Har Prompt Mein Ye 4 Cheezein Add Karo

Theory kafi hui. Ab ek simple, memorable rule jo tum har prompt mein apply kar sakte ho — starting today:

🚀 The 4-Element Upgrade Rule

AddRole
+
AddAudience
+
AddGoal
+
AddFormat
👉 Result automatically improve hoga — guaranteed, without exception

Ab aaj se jab bhi AI open karo — pehle ye 4 questions khud se pucho:

  1. Role: Kaun ban ke boluon?
    "Act as a [specific expert]" — jitna specific, utna better. Marketing expert, fitness coach, SEO writer, earnings consultant — jo relevant ho wo use karo.
  2. Audience: Kiske liye hai ye output?
    "Target: Indian college student with no money" ya "working professional with 2 hours daily." Audience define karne se AI automatically relevant examples aur context use karta hai.
  3. Goal: Kya result chahiye?
    Earning? Learning? Content creation? Specific task? Goal jitna clear hoga — AI ka direction utna clear hoga.
  4. Format: Kaisa output chahiye?
    Kitne points? Hinglish ya Hindi? Short ya detailed? Bullet points ya paragraph? First steps include karne hain? Word limit? Format define karo — consistent, deliverable output milega.
💡 30-Second Habit

Aaj se ye ek habit banao: pehle prompt type karo normally, phir pause karo, RAGF check karo. Missing elements add karo. Phir send karo. Ye 30 seconds extra har baar output quality 3-5x improve kar denge. Ek hafte mein ye automatic ho jaayega.
