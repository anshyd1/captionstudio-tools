2. Work in this update

  • Added five topic-specific white/red/gold hero illustrations (bio, boys, girls, Mahadev, reels) as WebP with JPG alternatives; updated image sitemap and bio social metadata.
  • Added quick navigation to bio styles; removed an unsupported conversion claim and corrected the bio generator label.
  • Added community CTA visibility and bio-navigation click instrumentation. Visibility means the CTA was at least 50% visible for one second, once per element per page load; this is not a Telegram join.
  • Removed four unrelated cricket-to-football redirects. Those retired paths should return 404 if no matching source page exists. Verify on production after deployment; if a genuinely relevant replacement is found, consider a targeted redirect.

Implementation does not guarantee higher rankings or traffic. New tracking must be validated after deploy in GA4 DebugView. No Pinterest pins were published as part of this update.
