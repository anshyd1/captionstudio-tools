📖 What Does "It's Coming Home" Actually Mean? (The Real Story)

In literal historical terms, modern Association Football rules were written and codified at the Freemasons' Tavern in London, England in 1863. Therefore, England is historically recognized as the "home" of football. When England hosted the Euro 1996 tournament, comedians David Baddiel and Frank Skinner teamed up with indie band The Lightning Seeds to write the official tournament anthem titled **"Three Lions."**

The iconic 1996 chorus references the Three Lions royal crest, the gleaming Jules Rimet World Cup trophy, and thirty years of historical heartbreak—all culminating in the famous refrain that football is coming home.

More importantly, unlike arrogant victory songs from other nations, **"It's Coming Home" is rooted in emotional vulnerability and self-deprecating irony.** English supporters sing it knowing full well their team has suffered decades of heartbreaking penalty shootout losses since their solo 1966 World Cup triumph. Ahead of the 2026 World Cup, fans hope this summer finally ends the wait.
