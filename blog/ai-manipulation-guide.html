🧪 Section 05

Prompt Engineering — Puri Detail Mein (Beginners ke liye)

Best prompt engineering tips for beginners — ek baar seekho, lifetime kaam aayega

Ek perfect prompt ke 5 essential components hote hain:

  1. Role — "Act as a viral Instagram content creator" — jitna specific, utna better
  2. Context — Topic, audience details, platform — AI ko sab batao
  3. Task — "Write 5 captions" — measurable aur specific hona chahiye
  4. Format — Word limit, language, numbered ya paragraph — define karo
  5. Constraints — "No hashtags", "Under 15 words" — kya nahi chahiye ye batao

Real Prompt Examples — Copy & Try Karo:

EXAMPLE 1 — Instagram Hook (Hinglish) 
Role: Act as viral Instagram reels scriptwriter
specializing in Hinglish content for Gen-Z India.

Context: Topic: morning routine for students.
Audience: college students, 18-22 years.
Platform: Instagram Reels.

Task: Write 5 scroll-stopping hook lines
for the first 3 seconds of a reel.

Format: Numbered list. Max 10 words each.
Hinglish. No hashtags.

Constraint: Don't use "amazing", "best", "ultimate".
Be raw and relatable.

// ChatGPT prompts for Instagram Hindi — perfect formula
EXAMPLE 2 — Email Subject Lines 
Role: Email marketing expert, 10 years in edtech India.

Context: Launching free AI tools course.
Target: working professionals 25-35. Goal: high open rate.

Task: Write 7 subject lines — curiosity + urgency.

Constraints: Under 50 chars each. No clickbait.
No ALL CAPS. No exclamation marks.
EXAMPLE 3 — Instagram Bio 
Role: Personal branding expert for Indian creators.

Context: Niche: AI tools + student earning.
Audience: college students wanting to earn online.
Vibe: friendly, aspirational, relatable.

Task: Write 5 Instagram bio options.

Format: Under 150 chars each.
Hinglish. 1 CTA line. Emojis sparingly.
📌 Pro Tip — Chain Prompting

Step 1: "Write 10 caption ideas for fitness niche"
Step 2: "Make caption #3 more emotional, add CTA"
Step 3: "Shorten to under 20 words, keep emotion"

Yahi method top copywriters use karte hain. 🎯

🚀 Section 08

Advanced Prompt Techniques — Level Up Karo

1. Few-Shot Prompting

AI ko examples do taaki wo tumhara exact style samjhe.

📝 Few-Shot Example

"Write captions in this exact style:
Example 1: Subah 5 baje uthna meri choice nahi thi — par ab hai. 💪
Example 2: Log hasenge. Haso. Phir dekho kaun aage hai.
Now write 5 more for: financial freedom for students"

2. Persona Assignment

"Act as a 22-year-old fitness influencer from Delhi who speaks Hinglish." Jitni specific identity — utna better brand voice output.

3. Negative Prompting

Sirf ye mat batao ki kya chahiye — kya bilkul nahi chahiye ye bhi batao. Output quality surprisingly improve hoti hai.

4. Iterative Refinement Loop

  1. Initial Broad Prompt — pehle ideas lo, quantity over quality
  2. Emotion/Tone Refine — "Make this more emotional"
  3. Length Optimize — "Shorten to 15 words, keep core message"
  4. Human Touch — apni voice, personal story add karo

⚠️ Section 09

7 Common Mistakes Jo Log Karte Hain

In mistakes ko avoid karo — output quality instantly improve hogi

  1. Vague Instructions
    "Kuch accha likho" — AI ke liye "accha" undefined hai. Hamesha specific raho.
  2. Context Skip Karna
    AI ko nahi pata tumhara niche, audience, tone. Ye sab define karo.
  3. Pehle Output Pe Settle Hona
    First output sirf starting point hai. Iterate karo.
  4. AI Output Directly Post Karna
    Human polish zaroor karo. Raw AI content easily detectable hota hai.
  5. Same Prompt Repeat Karna
    Experiment karo — alag angles try karo.
  6. Negative Constraints Ignore Karna
    "No emojis", "No hashtags" — ye quality control ke liye critical hain.
  7. System Mein Use Na Karna
    Random use se inconsistent results. Defined workflow banao.
